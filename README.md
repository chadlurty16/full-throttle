# Full Throttle

A text-and-stats-based, decision-driven motorsport career RPG. Start in karting, hobby stocks, dirt, Legends, Bandoleros, Spec Miata or F4, then work your way up through 46 series to NASCAR Cup, F1, IndyCar, IMSA, WEC, Formula E and more. It has race-weekend strategy, full career stats, silly seasons with contract negotiation, storylines, and team ownership.

## Play

Open `racing_career.html` in any browser. It runs offline and autosaves in the browser; use export/import in Settings to back up your career.

## Develop

- Source lives in `src/`. Run `./build.sh` to rebuild `racing_career.html`.
- Tests: `npm install`, then `node test/run_all.js` (about 4 minutes; add `--long` for the full run).

Real series, tracks, teams and drivers appear in a fictional story; nothing here depicts real events. A fictional-only mode is in Settings.
